Our R package [pcdreg](https://www.sundayu.me/pcdreg/) is now on [CRAN](https://cran.r-project.org/package=pcdreg). It implements the panel count regression methods of our [_Bernoulli_ paper](https://doi.org/10.3150/23-BEJ1713): install it with `install.packages("pcdreg")`.
